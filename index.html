How it works

The engines behind every decision

Twelve specialised engines run on each candidate trade — analysis, validation, then risk and execution.

DECISION PIPELINE live

ANALYSIS & SIGNAL

Confluence engine

Acts only when multiple independent signals agree on the same read.

Probability engine

Scores each setup's statistical edge from the outcomes of similar past conditions.

Market regime engine

Classifies trend, range, and volatility so the rules adapt to the current market.

Similarity engine

Matches the current situation to historical ones and checks how they resolved.

Smart Money Concepts

Reads order blocks, liquidity, and market-structure breaks.

Multi-timeframe alignment

Checks 15M, 1H, and 4H agree — and flags counter-trend setups that need higher approval.

Liquidity & institutional

Weighs where liquidity sits and whether there's adequate institutional confirmation.

DECISION & VALIDATION

Parameter Testing engine

Every element of the strategy is tested before it is trusted. What gets tested:

  • ▸ Indicator parameters
  • ▸ Risk thresholds
  • ▸ Similarity thresholds
  • ▸ Entry conditions
  • ▸ Stop-loss logic
  • ▸ Take-profit logic
  • ▸ Trade-management rules
  • ▸ Market filters

Backtesting engine

Runs the validated ruleset across up to five years of market data to measure behaviour across cycles.

RISK & EXECUTION

Risk assessment engine

Sets position size, stop distance, and reward-to-risk before you commit.

Risk filter & guardrails

Enforces daily loss limits, max trades, and maximum account exposure.

Execution & discipline

Holds you to the plan at entry — blocking revenge and oversized trades.
