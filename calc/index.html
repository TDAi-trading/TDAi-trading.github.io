3Does liquidation get in the way?

The stop must come first. If liquidation is nearer, the trade is already lost.

Liquidation being far away is not spare room: the stop still fills first, and a bigger position makes that stop cost more. To use the room, raise the loss you accept in step 2 — or move the stop closer.
